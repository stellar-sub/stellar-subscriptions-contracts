# Threat Model

This model covers the plan, registry and subscription Soroban contracts. The
primary assets are subscriber funds and consent, subscription and plan state,
and the integrity of registry records. Authorization assumes the Soroban host
correctly verifies signatures and enforces contract-call rules. The plan and
registry contracts are bookkeeping and configuration dependencies; only the
subscription contract moves funds.

| Actor | Assets and entry points | Mitigations and evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Subscriber | Funds, spending consent and private subscription state. Calls `subscribe`, `subscribe_until`, `pause`, `resume`, `cancel` and `refresh_allowance`. | Subscriber signatures are required for consent and subscriber actions; see `subscribe_requires_the_subscribers_signature`, `cancel_requires_the_subscribers_signature_not_the_merchants`, and `pause_and_resume_by_non_subscriber_are_rejected` in [`auth.rs`](../contracts/subscription/src/test/auth.rs). Amount, interval, cap and expiry are validated by `subscribe_rejects_a_non_positive_amount`, `subscribe_rejects_a_zero_interval`, `subscribe_rejects_a_cap_below_one_period` ([`validation.rs`](../contracts/subscription/src/test/validation.rs)) and `an_end_ledger_that_is_not_in_the_future_is_refused` ([`expiry.rs`](../contracts/subscription/src/test/expiry.rs)). A malicious subscriber can create large histories that consume resources; see the documented large-history limit in [`SECURITY.md`](../SECURITY.md). |
| Merchant | Subscriber-authorized funds and subscription schedule. Calls `charge`; publishes and deactivates plans through `create_plan` and `deactivate_plan`. | Merchant identity, CAP, INTERVAL and REVOCATION are checked by `charge`; evidence is in [`invariant_cap.rs`](../contracts/subscription/src/test/invariant_cap.rs), [`invariant_interval.rs`](../contracts/subscription/src/test/invariant_interval.rs), [`invariant_revocation.rs`](../contracts/subscription/src/test/invariant_revocation.rs), fuzz test `invariants_hold_for_any_sequence_of_actions`, and [`verify-testnet.sh`](../scripts/verify-testnet.sh). Cross-subscriber access is tested by `charge_by_an_address_that_is_not_the_merchant_is_rejected` ([`auth.rs`](../contracts/subscription/src/test/auth.rs)). Plan ownership is tested by `another_merchant_cannot_deactivate_a_plan` ([`plan/src/test.rs`](../contracts/plan/src/test.rs)); exact plan terms are checked by `terms_that_differ_from_the_plan_are_rejected` ([`integration.rs`](../contracts/subscription/src/test/integration.rs]). The contract cannot protect subscribers from deceptive off-chain marketing; subscribers must review merchant and plan terms before consenting. |
| Admin | One-time contract links and initial configuration. Calls `initialize`, `set_plan_contract`, `set_registry`, `set_writers` and the plan registry link. | Links/writers are admin-only, one-time and closed after first use. Subscription-link restrictions are tested by `links_are_set_once_and_only_by_the_admin` and `links_cannot_be_added_after_the_first_subscription` ([`integration.rs`](../contracts/subscription/src/test/integration.rs)); plan and registry restrictions are tested by `set_registry_is_admin_only_once_and_refused_after_the_first_plan` ([`plan/src/test.rs`](../contracts/plan/src/test.rs)) and `only_the_admin_can_set_writers_and_only_once` ([`registry/src/test.rs`](../contracts/registry/src/test.rs)). Admin has no subscription-management path; auth tests include `every_state_changing_function_rejects_a_call_with_no_signature` ([`auth.rs`](../contracts/subscription/src/test/auth.rs)). |
| Token | Approved allowance and subscriber funds; called by `subscribe`, `refresh_allowance`, `cancel` and `charge` (`approve` / `transfer_from`). | Failed token transfers leave state unchanged (`a_transfer_from_panic_leaves_cap_schedule_and_status_unchanged`, [`hostile_token.rs`](../contracts/subscription/src/test/hostile_token.rs)); cancellation remains final despite a hostile token (`a_token_that_panics_cannot_stop_the_subscriber_from_cancelling`, same file). Reentrant `transfer_from` callbacks fail at the Soroban host boundary and cannot double charge (`token_callback_cannot_reenter_charge_or_double_charge`, [`reentrant_token.rs`](../contracts/subscription/src/test/reentrant_token.rs)). A token may misreport balances or exhaust transaction resources; this is a documented trust limit in [`SECURITY.md`](../SECURITY.md), not a behavior these tests prove. |
| Registry | Integrity of mirrored subscription/plan records and aggregate statistics. Exposes `register_plan`, `register_subscription` and `update_subscription` to configured writers. | Writer authorization is tested by `only_the_subscription_contract_can_register_subscriptions` and `only_the_plan_contract_can_register_plans` ([`registry/src/test.rs`](../contracts/registry/src/test.rs)). Duplicate records, decreasing totals and edits to final records are rejected by `registering_the_same_subscription_twice_is_rejected`, `total_charged_can_never_decrease` and `final_subscriptions_never_change_again` (same file). A broken registry may block subscribe, charge, pause or resume, but cannot block cancel; see `a_misconfigured_registry_blocks_subscribe_instead_of_losing_the_record` and `cancel_with_a_broken_registry_reports_registry_updated_false_and_still_cancels` ([`integration.rs`](../contracts/subscription/src/test/integration.rs)). |

## Assumptions and limits

- A subscriber chooses a token and trusts its balance and allowance semantics.
  A malicious token can misreport its own balances; see the token limitation in
  [`SECURITY.md`](../SECURITY.md).
- The Soroban host enforces authorization and rejects reentrant contract calls.
  The subscription regression test covers a token callback into `charge`, but
  does not prove safety against host implementation defects.
- No third-party audit has been completed. The contracts are deployed on
  Testnet only; this is documented in [`SECURITY.md`](../SECURITY.md).
